Quantum Computing, Complexity, and Control
QCCC-Kanazawa 2026

Kanazawa University, Kakuma-machi, Kanazawa 920-1192, Japan
June 30 – July 3, 2026

Last update: June 09, 2026

QCCC-Kanazawa 2026 is a small workshop on quantum computing, quantum dynamics, complexity, and quantum control, organized in the spirit of the main QCCC2026 conference. The workshop aims to bring together researchers working on various aspects of quantum science and technology, including quantum information, many-body physics, quantum algorithms, and non-equilibrium dynamics.

Scope

The workshop focuses on recent developments in quantum science, including:

  • Quantum computing and quantum algorithms
  • Quantum complexity and Krylov dynamics
  • Quantum control and shortcuts to adiabaticity
  • Non-equilibrium quantum dynamics
  • Quantum information and entanglement
  • Mathematical and numerical methods in quantum physics
